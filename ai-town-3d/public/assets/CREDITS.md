## Scanned people

Renderpeople official free samples: https://renderpeople.com/free-3d-people/

- Nathan animated 003 walking: converted from the official FBX sample.
- Sophia animated 003 idling: converted from the official FBX sample.
- Manuel animated 001 dancing: converted from the official FBX sample.
- rp_posed_00178_29: official GLB posed-person sample.

Original source downloads:
https://renderpeople.com/sample/free/renderpeople_free_animated_people_FBX.zip
https://renderpeople.com/sample/free/rp_posed_00178_29_GLB.zip

Renderpeople advertises these samples as free to download and use commercially, without registration; their standard terms still apply. Assets are used within this private LAN scene preview, not sold or represented as original models. Six separate Renderpeople scans and two Rocketbox avatars are now used, one per resident. Their original identities are unrelated to the fictional UI names.

Additional Renderpeople free rigged scans: Eric rigged 001, Carla rigged 001, Claudia rigged 002. Official source: https://renderpeople.com/sample/free/renderpeople_free_rigged_people_FBX.zip

Microsoft Rocketbox Male_Adult_08 and Female_Adult_01: https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox . MIT license, preserved in Rocketbox-LICENSE.txt. These are realistic rigged avatars rather than new photogrammetry scans. FBX and textures converted locally; no account or paid purchase required.

## Environment

Poly Haven CC0 PBR textures: concrete_pavement, asphalt_02, brick_wall_001, forest_ground_04, brown_mud_03, bark_brown_01. Maps at 1K. https://polyhaven.com/license

Poly Haven CC0 models, reduced for browser use: island_tree_01, tree_stump_01, rock_moss_set_01. https://polyhaven.com/models

Grass photograph: Three.js examples textures/terrain/grasslight-big.jpg. Original map and resident sprite references retained from the user's AI Town installation (https://github.com/a16z-infra/ai-town); underlying asset rights remain with their original authors.

Sunset HDR: venice_sunset_1k.hdr distributed in Three.js examples, originally Poly Haven (HDRI Haven).

Buildings, street furniture, procedural fallback materials and foliage geometry are generated by this project. The street is not a scanned location.
